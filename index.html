Automatic model: David Lorenz Light Pollution Atlas, VIIRS 2025. The atlas models zenith brightness, not observed Bortle class. The Bortle equivalent is an approximate conversion; actual sky conditions can differ.

What goes into your score?

Clouds 40% · Moon 15% · sky darkness 15% · humidity 10% · wind 10% · visibility 10%.

Daylight, rain and overcast skies limit the score. This is a planning guide, not a success probability. Visibility does not measure astronomical seeing.
